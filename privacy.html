Privacy Policy

AntonGPT · Last updated August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AntonGPT (“the GPT”) handles information when you use its external Actions.

Information processed

When you ask the GPT to use an Action, the information needed to complete that request may be sent to the connected external API. This can include the text of your request, commands or parameters you provide, and technical information necessary to process the request.

Do not submit passwords, authentication tokens, financial information, or other sensitive personal information unless the requested function explicitly requires it and you trust the service.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the requested functionality, operate and secure the service, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse. It is not used to build advertising profiles and is not sold.

Storage and retention

The service does not intentionally create long-term profiles from Action requests. Limited operational and security logs may be retained for only as long as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, security, and legal obligations. Infrastructure providers may also retain technical logs under their own policies.

Sharing

Information is not shared with third parties except with infrastructure providers needed to operate the API, when required by law, or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

OpenAI and third-party services

Your use of ChatGPT and the GPT is also governed by OpenAI’s applicable terms and privacy policy. External services used to host or deliver an Action may process limited technical data under their own policies.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can choose not to invoke external Actions or not to provide optional information. To ask a privacy question or request deletion of information held by the Action service, use the contact method below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the GPT or its Actions change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the owner through GitHub: @tonygit33.
