# Mobile listing card QA — 2026-08-02

Regression reproduced from the iPhone screenshot supplied by the product owner.

Observed failure:
- the detail media container was capped at 480px;
- the 17-photo thumbnail grid overflowed that container;
- property text and facts began underneath the overflowing thumbnails;
- links and facts became unreadable and difficult to tap.

Release acceptance criteria:
- main photo keeps a stable 4:3 ratio;
- thumbnails use a single horizontally scrollable row;
- gallery height remains in normal document flow;
- property copy starts below the gallery;
- modal itself scrolls vertically within the iPhone viewport;
- close control remains reachable;
- facts and actions remain readable and tappable.
